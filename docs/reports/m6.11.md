## Checks

- Typecheck, lint (2 old warnings), branding and origin checks. Vitest: 37 files, 198 tests passed (3 new in `m610-feedback`: In a call, the group picture, a deleted message).
- E2E, the whole suite: 56 passed, 3 failed. `m4-mesh` (8 PCs on one machine) is the failure this PC has always had. The other two were mine: after the People rail, `expectPerson` did not return to the chat rail when the page had not shown its rail yet (m1-chat), and a test checked the People list with Settings still open over it (m6-scale). Both fixed and re-run green. One more test, the relay meeting in `m6-scale`, failed once in that re-run and passed alone and in the full run: a load flake, not seen twice.
- New e2e (`m610-ui`, `m69-ui`): the People rail, one dot and no status message in the chat title, a group picture seen by another member, forwarding with a note, the controls bar not moving when a chip appears, the audio worklet running, the forced update window.
- All the existing e2e that opened a chat from the people list were changed to go through the People rail (`clickPerson`, `expectPerson` in `app.ts`).

## Not verified

- Noise: not listened to; the cause of the static (main-thread audio) is argued, not measured.
- Dark mode: reaction counts and scroll bars were not looked at in a dark-mode screenshot.
- The SmartScreen workaround was not tried on a PC that shows the warning (no such PC here); the installer and the `.cmd` were not run.
- The update share (not reachable from here) and an update installed by the app.
- Older builds: the "In a call" flag, group pictures and the People rail data are ignored by them; chunk requests are still limited to 50 a second on them.
- No version bump, no release build, nothing committed.
