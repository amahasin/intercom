## What was built

- **Protocol:**
  - Bodies for `call.invite`, `call.ringing`, `call.accept`, `call.decline`, `call.sdp` (signed, with the DTLS fingerprint), `call.ice`, `call.hangup` and `call.state` (the replicated roster), under capability `calls.mesh`.
  - 12 IPC commands and 4 events.
  - Device settings and a missed-call Activity kind.
- **Core:**
  - Call manager: one call at a time, a 30 s ring, auto-decline (busy, Do not disturb, full) with the reason, invitations to more people mid-call, and the replicated roster.
  - Signed SDP relay and ICE relay.
  - Drops, history (`calls` table), missed calls in Activity with toasts, and Busy presence while in a call.
- **Desktop (main process):**
  - Camera, microphone and screen-capture permission for the app's own pages only.
  - The screen and window list with thumbnails, and a capture-excluded border around a shared screen.
  - Call toasts, no background throttling, real host ICE candidates, and `--fake-media` for tests.
- **UI:**
  - Call engine: mesh `RTCPeerConnection`s with perfect negotiation, a heartbeat data channel, speaking detection, video limits by size, screen share, hold and mute, and retried device opening.
  - Call stage with a tile grid, a shared-screen layout, and controls (mute, camera, share, hold, add people, minimise, leave); a mini view.
  - Incoming-call card with a generated ring tone; reason notices for declined calls.
  - Screen picker; Calls rail with history and call back; device settings with preview, level meter and a 5-second test call; call buttons in 1:1 and group chats.

## Tests

- 121 unit and integration tests (Vitest, 25 files, 96 s). New: 7 call-signaling tests covering:
  - ring and answer;
  - signed SDP and ICE relay, and a forged SDP signature rejected;
  - Do not disturb, busy, unanswered after 30 s and cancelled-while-ringing;
  - group roster, add, leave and drop;
  - mute in the roster, and Busy presence while in a call.
- 18 Electron end-to-end tests (Playwright, 2.8 min), including the four new call tests: 1:1 with share and glare, Do not disturb, cable pull, and the 8-person mesh.
- `pnpm build` (typecheck, bundle, no network origins) and lint pass.
- `pnpm dist` builds `OfficeMesh-Setup-0.4.0-x64.exe` and `-arm64.exe`, and the packaged smoke test passes.

## Bugs found and fixed during M4

- **A dropped participant was never told.** The drop was broadcast only to remaining members, so the dropped PC's call went on. It is now also sent to them directly.
- **Camera toggles by renegotiating did not survive glare in a three-way call.** After simultaneous toggles, one link stopped delivering frames. Toggles now swap the track without renegotiating, and glare is exercised (and handled) by simultaneous screen-share starts.
- **A camera that stopped by itself was never noticed.** In the first 30-minute mesh run, one PC's camera track ended and it kept "sending" a dead track (seven PCs decoded about 36,000 frames, one only 661). The engine now checks its devices every half second and reopens a lost camera or microphone every 3 s until it works, swapping the new track onto every connection.
- **Turning the camera back on could fail with "Requested device not found"** while the capture service restarted. Opening devices now retries briefly, and a camera that fails to turn on stays "on" until the device check opens it.
- **Chromium's synthetic camera made call tests flaky.** In about half the two-app runs one app's synthetic camera stopped for good. Test builds now draw a test-pattern camera on a canvas instead: 16 out of 16 repeated runs of the call tests passed afterwards, against 2 out of 6 before.
- **Killing only Electron's main process left the renderer sending media and heartbeats**, so the first cable-pull test never dropped anyone. The test now kills the whole process tree, as a pulled cable or power loss would.

## Known limitations

- **System audio:** sharing system audio (loopback) is not implemented; the plan marks it optional.
- **Hold:** hold mutes this user's camera and microphone and silences the others' audio locally; the other participants keep talking among themselves.
- **No relay:** calls above 8 people with video or 12 audio-only need M6's host relay.
