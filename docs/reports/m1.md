## What was built

- **Protocol:** sync bodies (`sync.summary`, `sync.events` with `more`, `event.push`, `event.ack`), capability-to-type mapping, and 14 new IPC commands and events.
- **Discovery:**
  - Signed beacons over UDP multicast (TTL 1) plus subnet broadcast, on every IPv4 interface, re-polled for network changes.
  - Verification cache.
  - `bye` on quit and sleep, flushed before the socket closes.
  - Clock-skew rejection with a UI warning.
- **Peer directory:** trust on first use, "new device" flag (including your own name), safety-phrase verification, sticky addresses, batched UI updates.
- **Presence:** manual states; auto-Away on idle (5 min) or lock for Available and Busy; sleep and wake handling.
- **Peer links:**
  - WebSocket inside mutual TLS 1.3, pinned by the key hash.
  - `hello` and capabilities; one link per pair (the lower userId's dial wins).
  - Ping/pong, idle close, abuse limits with a 60 s ban, reconnect backoff, request/response.
  - Random-port fallback.
- **Sync:**
  - Signed `msg.post` events with a hybrid logical clock.
  - Gap-free application with buffering; duplicates ignored (exactly once).
  - Summary-based catch-up on every link-up.
  - Live push and acks driving Sending / Waiting for (name) / Delivered.
  - The outbox survives restarts.
- **Desktop:**
  - powerMonitor idle, lock, sleep and wake.
  - Windows Firewall check (unelevated) and one-click elevated fix.
  - Public-network detection; installer and uninstaller firewall hooks; `firewall.ps1` for IT.
- **UI:**
  - Chat list: chats plus everyone on the network, online first, filter with Ctrl+N, virtualised.
  - Conversation view: virtualised list, Enter to send, delivery status, offline note.
  - Profile card with safety phrase, accept and verify.
  - Presence menu, profile editor, and firewall / Public-network / clock banners.
- **Sim:** full peers on the real network (`--echo`), and `--lite=N` colleagues that beacon and serve profiles over real TLS from one process.

## Tests

- 76 unit and integration tests (Vitest), including real-UDP/TLS tests pinned to loopback.
- 10 Electron end-to-end tests (Playwright), on a private loopback LAN with their own ports.

## Bugs found and fixed during M1

- The quit `bye` beacon was cancelled by the immediate socket close. The e2e test found it; the simulated tests could not.
- Profile retries toward unreachable peers, plus re-parsing the TLS key on every dial, pushed beacon handling to 3.5% CPU for 200 peers. It is now 0.8%.
- `percentCPUUsage` under-reported core CPU by about 32× on this PC (it is normalised across cores). The test now uses cumulative CPU seconds.
- After **Accept** on a profile card, the card stayed on screen (a Fluent motion stall). It is worked around.
