## The three that needed care

- **File speed (8).** Measured before changing anything. 768 MB took 65 s because the transfer ran at about 280 MB/s for two seconds, then the receiver closed the link for "rate limit exceeded" (every chunk request is a text frame; the limit is 50 a second) and refused it for 60 s. The fix counts chunk requests apart, asks in whole lots, keeps one read handle per served file, reads ahead, and stops copying each megabyte twice. A new test (`m69-files-speed.test.ts`) moves 768 MB and fails under 60 MB/s.
- **Random removals (5).** A participant was dropped after 6 s without a heartbeat, which removes them from the call for everyone and ends their call. Likely causes found in the code: a link made by an offer that arrives before the roster listing its sender was dropped as a stranger and never rebuilt (then the joiner timed out and dropped the other person), a whiteboard picture holding up the heartbeat, and a busy window. Now anything arriving counts as hearing from them, a stalled window forgives the silence, a quiet link has its connection restarted, and the strangers' links are kept for 20 s. A dead PC is still removed within 10 s (7 s of nothing at all). Covered by unit tests of the decision (`judgeLink`); the existing "cable pulled" e2e covers the dead-PC side.
- **Noise (6, 7).** The setting did not take effect because the old microphone stayed open while the new one was opened (Chromium shares one capture per device and fixes its processing at first open). It is let go first now, checked after opening, and reopened whenever the setting changes in any window. The gate in "High" is about 40 dB down instead of 24, ignores click-like bursts, needs a few blocks of voice-like sound, and keeps open only for sound above a third of the user's usual level.

## Checks

- Typecheck, lint (2 old warnings), branding and origin checks.
- Vitest, new: `m69-call-liveness` (3), `m69-files-speed` (1), noise gate (3 more), group-call card (2), update folder default (1). Full run at the end: 36 files, 195 tests passed.
- E2E: a 41-test batch (chat, rich messaging, groups, calls, meetings, call windows, remote control, the UI specs M6.1 to M6.7, and the 4 new ones in `m69-ui.spec.ts`) gave 39 passed and 2 failed. Both were found to be mine and fixed: the pulled-cable test (a first probe of the incoming media counted as proof the peer was alive, so a dead PC took about 10 s to be dropped instead of under 8; the limit is now 7 s) and my own update test (a text file is not a valid unsigned program). Both passed on a second run. The rest of the batch was not run again after those two fixes.
- Screenshots checked: the People pane with the count and "Add people", the one-row status chip, the "Update required" window.

## Not verified

- Real microphones: the gate was tuned on synthetic levels; nobody listened to it. A neural suppressor (RNNoise) would be better still; it needs a new dependency and was not added unasked.
- Real calls with five or six PCs, with a whiteboard picture, over a busy network: the removals were argued from the code.
- The update share: not reachable from here, and no installer was run. This build is not code-signed, so the update check says "unsigned".
- File speed on a real 1 Gbit/s link and real disks (the measure is loopback on one PC; the real figure will be about 110 MB/s at best).
- A mixed office: an older build keeps the 50 frames a second limit, ignores `end` and the call chat, and cannot take part in a call card.
- No version bump, no release build, nothing committed.
