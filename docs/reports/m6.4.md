| #   | Question                                         | Answer and change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Visual inconsistency (offline note under a chat) | The note was an inline `Text` with side padding, so its first line was indented and the rest was not.
