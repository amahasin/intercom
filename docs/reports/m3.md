## What was built

- **Protocol:**
  - Events `group.create` and `conv.rename`.
  - Bodies for groups, teams, membership, roles, channels, polls, threads and announcements.
  - Member entries carry public keys.
  - Wire `event.batch`, `team.fetch` and `team.data`, plus public teams in `hello`, all under capability `teams.v1`.
  - 20 IPC commands and 2 events.
- **Core:**
  - Self-certifying group, team and channel ids.
  - A membership timeline with the plan's authorisation rules, and author-was-a-member checks in the materialiser, with re-materialisation when membership history changes.
  - Holding events until their conversation or author can be placed, and accepting invitations.
  - Forwarding with `sentTo`; batched pushes.
  - Anti-entropy on link up, pending, behind and rotating gossip; serving former members up to their removal; sparse membership events.
  - Public team discovery and join; channels and private channel scopes; threads, announcements, polls and the files list.
  - Read receipts in groups of up to 20; Activity and toasts for replies in your thread.
- **UI:**
  - New group chat with a people picker; a group header with member management, rename and leave.
  - Author names in group messages.
  - Teams rail: create team, join a public team, team and channel menus (add, rename, archive, leave).
  - Channel view with Posts (cards, announcements, expandable threads with reply composers), Files and Members tabs.
  - Poll composer and live poll card; Files rail; Chat and Teams unread badges.
- **Tests:** M3 integration tests, the randomised sync harness (`tests/integration/fuzz.ts`, `fuzz-all.ts`, `fuzz-cli.ts` to replay one seed), regression seeds sharded over six test files, and an M3 end-to-end test.

## Tests

- 114 unit and integration tests (Vitest, 24 files, 189 s). This includes the nine regression seeds at full size and two quick fresh seeds.
- 14 Electron end-to-end tests (Playwright, 1.2 min). The new M3 test drives a group chat, a poll, a team, an announcement, a thread reply, the Members tab and a new channel across two app instances.
- `pnpm build` (typecheck, bundle, no network origins) and lint pass.
- `pnpm dist` builds `OfficeMesh-Setup-0.3.0-x64.exe` (104 MB) and `-arm64.exe` (98 MB). The packaged smoke test passes.

## Bugs found and fixed during M3

The randomised test found five ways the first design failed to converge:

1. **A removed member never learnt of its own removal** when the push was lost, because anti-entropy compared only conversations both sides currently belonged to. Former members now sync up to the moment they left.
2. **A member added while partitioned never learnt of the addition**, the mirror case. Summaries now cover every conversation either side has shared with the peer.
3. **Two groups of peers whose links survived a silent outage never compared notes.** Nothing was pending between them and no link went down and up. A rotating background gossip every 15 s fixes this.
4. **A removed member was re-added by an owner whom a concurrent change had already demoted.** The member could not see the demotion and believed it was still in the team. Former members now receive membership events up to a later re-addition, which are kept even when they arrive ahead of the author's earlier posts.
5. **Pushes lost on a link that had not yet noticed the outage were only resent at the next link-up.** Peers with pending events now get a summary every 10 s.

Other bugs:

- **A burst of 500 posts was sent as 500 frames to each member**, which tripped the 50-frames-per-second abuse limit and got the sender banned. Pushes are now batched per task.
- **A genesis relayed by someone other than its author was held forever**, because the relay check ran before the conversation had any members. A genesis is self-certifying and skips the relay check.
- **A member who had just joined a team was not sent the channel history.** They now ask a few online members as soon as the join is applied, after their own join has been pushed.
- **Public teams were advertised only when a link opened.** They are now re-advertised with a repeated `hello` when they change.
- **Random identity keys per run made failing seeds unreproducible.** The harness now reuses one set of keys.

## Known limitations

- **Large control logs:** joining a public team fetches at most 500 control-log events. Larger logs arrive through normal sync after joining.
- **Votes racing a close:** a vote cast concurrently with its poll's close is dropped on every PC (the app refuses votes on a poll it already knows is closed).
- **Stale public team names:** the public team list shows names as last advertised.
