## Item 3 in detail

- **Instant.** Sharing from disk now only reads the file's size. A 6 GB file is offered in under 1.5 s (tested), a 2 GB one in under 2 s. Before, the whole file was read first (minutes), and again on the receiver at the end.
- **How.** The file's id is random and the `manifest` is 64 zeros (`UNHASHED_MANIFEST` in the protocol package). The receiver skips the chunk list and the per-chunk check, takes the file from its sender only, and checks the size at the end. Pasted and snipped pictures are still fingerprinted.
- **Offline colleague.** Before: a file that reached a PC more than 10 minutes after it was sent counted as history and needed a click; a download waiting for its sender was forgotten when the app closed. Now: anything sent since the PC last ran is fetched as soon as the message reaches it (tested with a clock jumped 3 hours ahead, and the test fails if the new rule is removed), and a wanted download is kept in the database and started again after a restart (tested). The sender's card says "Waiting for X to come online". An e2e test closes Bob's app, sends a file, reopens Bob's app and sees it arrive by itself.
- **Lost, by choice.** De-duplication by content; detection of a disk bit-flip; fetching from a third colleague who already has the file. See `docs/decisions.md`.
- **Compatibility.** Older builds (0.9.x and below) cannot receive a file from this build (they look for a chunk list that does not exist). Update everyone.
